# Leon Wang

**AI Evaluation · News Editing · Editorial Design**

I'm a newspaper editor and AI evaluator based in Philadelphia. My work combines editorial judgment, careful quality review, and hands-on page production.

## AI evaluation and review

- Compare AI responses side by side (A/B evaluation), assessing accuracy, instruction-following, clarity, and usefulness.
- Review response quality and explain judgments with clear, evidence-based feedback.
- Evaluate reasoning and multimodal outputs, with attention to whether the result matches the source and the task.

## News editing and page production

- Edit news copy with attention to facts, names, numbers, attribution, and meaning.
- Produce newspaper layouts in Adobe InDesign, balancing readability, hierarchy, and space.
- Fit and refine copy within established page designs without losing essential information.

## Tools I work with

Adobe InDesign · Photoshop · Illustrator · Canva · WordPress
